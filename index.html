App

Mic Control lives in the tray, next to the clock. Minimize or close this window and it keeps running: your sound, mute and monitor stay on. Click the tray icon to come back, right-click it to mute, monitor or quit.

Start with WindowsStarts quietly in the tray when you log in. Your sound works in every app either way.

Tips

For guitar, zero delay: the Focusrite's Direct Monitor button plays the dry inputs straight to its headphone jack. Turn it off while using Monitor here, or you hear both. Wired headphones beat wireless ones, which add their own delay on top.

Keep the Focusrite gain knob and your distance to the PodMic the same, since the presets depend on them. Talk into the top of the PodMic, 3 to 5 cm away. Leave the Windows recording level at 100%, and turn off "automatically adjust microphone" in Discord, Teams and Zoom.

How it works

Everything here runs inside Windows' audio engine (Equalizer APO) on the Focusrite input. Discord, OBS, games and every other app get the finished sound, even with this window closed.

Preview and Apply

Changes play live while you tweak. Apply keeps them. Revert or closing the window without Apply puts back the last applied sound. Mute and the Guitar switch work instantly.

Hear yourself

Monitor in the bottom bar plays your mic and guitar, exactly as apps hear them, into your headphones with the lowest delay Windows allows (Settings shows it). Test records 5 seconds and plays it back, which is the best way to judge your sound.

Order of the effects

Mic: boost → low cut → AI noise suppression → gate → EQ → warmth → de-esser → compressor → your effects → limiter. Guitar: boost → low cut → gate → amp tone → sustain → your effects, then mixed with your voice.
